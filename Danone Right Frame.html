How to Log In

Login Instructions & Support

👔 Danone Employees

Use the Login with Danone SSO button with your corporate email address (@danone.com or @external.danone.com).

Facing password issues? Visit Danone SSO Portal

👤 All Other Users

Enter your username and password in the login form on the right side.

If you don't have credentials yet, please contact your administrator.

❓ Unable to Log In?

Contact your administrator for assistance with account access or password reset.
